About

A Tampa Bay crew in blue shirts and white vans

Momentum AC & Electric has provided licensed HVAC and electrical service to the Tampa Bay area since 2016, from offices in Tampa, St. Petersburg and New Port Richey.

Every technician is background checked and factory trained, and the standard is simple: diagnose it right the first time, explain it in plain language and fix it for a fair price.

Reviewers call the work thorough and the team respectful of their homes, and the page you are reading carries 1,688 of them.

  • Explained plainlyYou hear what was wrong and get tips to avoid a repeat.
