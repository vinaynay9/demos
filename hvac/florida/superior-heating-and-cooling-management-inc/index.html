About

Family-owned, working Tampa Bay since 1991

Superior Heating and Cooling Management is a family-owned and operated HVAC company based at 785 Dunbar Ave in Oldsmar. They have served Tampa Bay for over 35 years with repair, maintenance and new unit installations.

They are a certified Rheem Pro Partner and hold an A+ rating with the Better Business Bureau. The team works in Hillsborough, Pinellas, Pasco, Polk and Hernando Counties.

Customers ask for Shawn by name. One family has worked with him for two years, and another says he took the time to explain their contract and how long their unit should last.
