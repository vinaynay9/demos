About

Family-owned, off Linebaugh Avenue

Home Therapist was started by Aridel Morales and his son Richard, and is owned by Aridel, Richard and Alejandro Morales. The shop is at W Linebaugh Ave in Tampa, and the crew drives out to homes across Hillsborough, Pinellas and Pasco counties.

Customers name the people who came: Jandiel who explained how to keep a unit from freezing in the hot months, Lester the plumber who gave a fair quote, Mike on a water heater swap, and Aridel on a mini-split install.
