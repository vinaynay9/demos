The crew

A technician's dream, now a family company

Rolando's HVAC was started in Florida by a technician named Rolando Mojarrieta, who wanted to give his community the best heating, ventilation and air conditioning service. As word spread, he brought in skilled people who share his values, and the company grew into the family-owned team it is today.

The office is at 5117 W Rio Vista Ave in Tampa, with a teal awning out front and white service vans and trucks parked beside it. Customers name Jorge, who took room measurements and talked through load and air supply, and George, who traced a lightning surge to its cause before turning the breaker back on.
