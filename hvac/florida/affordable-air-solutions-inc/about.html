About

A Tampa crew, growing since 2010

Affordable Air Solutions is a veteran-owned air conditioning contractor on West Crenshaw Street in Tampa. The owner, Patrick Craig, is a Gulf War veteran with nearly 20 years in HVAC, including 10 with his own company.

The team brings more than 40 years of combined HVAC experience. The business has grown by word of mouth since 2010. There are no commissioned salespeople, and when you call, someone answers and can talk prices over the phone.
