About

Family owned on W Pearl Ave

Sunstate Mechanical Contractors has been family owned and operated in Tampa since 1996. The crew has over 40 combined years in air conditioning and heating, and every technician is background checked.

They are an authorized Trane dealer but service and repair any brand. Customers talk about Joe by name: one had him maintain a home system for over a decade, another had him rebuild their AC setup after Hurricanes Helene and Milton.
