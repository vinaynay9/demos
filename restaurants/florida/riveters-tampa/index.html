Meet the crew

A tribute to Rosie, with TVs

Riveters is an eclectic bar and restaurant with a nostalgic feel at 2301 N Dale Mabry Hwy, near Tampa's Midtown. Black and red outside, a star on the sign, and a long bar inside with a wall of screens for game day. The theme honors the era that changed the role of women in work and society.

Guests describe a place where everyone says hi when you walk in. One reviewer named Taylor for the drink she recommended, another walked in before a concert and found indoor and outdoor seating with shade. Big busy days, like World Cup final day, still got fast food and good service.
