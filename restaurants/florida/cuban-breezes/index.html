Our story

Everybody's Cuban restaurant

Cuban Breezes is a Cuban restaurant on West Hillsborough Avenue in Tampa. The sign out front says it all: Everybody's Cuban Restaurant.

Guests describe a great atmosphere, a dining room that stays busy, and staff who are attentive and friendly. Order a soft drink and the cup goes home with you.

  • Sandwich firstReviewers call the Cuban sandwich a must.
  • Full platesBlack beans, maduros, lechon and a big burger all get praise.
