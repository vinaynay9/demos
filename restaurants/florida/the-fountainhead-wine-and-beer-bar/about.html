About

Tucked into Highland Park

The Fountainhead is a casually upscale neighborhood bar in the Highland Park area of Westchase, in Northwest Tampa. Locals and friends come for a good glass of wine or a cold beer.

Inside you will find a tufted banquette, pendant lights, leather chairs, a bar with taps and TVs for the game. Guests call it small and intimate, with staff who have time for you. Reviewers mention Camryn by name.

  • 1Small and intimateA guest said it was not crowded, so you get the staff's attention.
  • 2Not-too-carefully curatedA wine list kept by people who just love wine.
