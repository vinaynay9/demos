Our story

Guido's table, Gino's kitchen

Donatello was founded in 1984 by the late Guido Tiozzo, who came from Venice. Today his son, Gino Tiozzo, carries the tradition on, with pasta made by hand every day and recipes that travel from Venice down to Naples and the Amalfi coast.

Guests tell the rest: a greeting at the door like family, waiters proud of their work, roses for the family at the end of the night, a chorus of opera singers on a Tuesday and live music in the lounge. Valet is part of the evening.

  • Made by handFresh pasta every day, from Venetian seafood to Roman classics.
  • Like familyGreeted at the door, looked after by proud, professional waiters.
  • Music and roomA live jazz lounge and a banquet room for private events.
