Our story

The people behind Nene's.

The pit

Eastern Europe, in Tampa

Nene's Restaurant & Bar is on West El Prado Boulevard in Tampa and cooks the dishes of Georgia and Ukraine: khinkali, khachapuri, plov, borscht, vareniki and more. The sign says Ukrainian Georgian cuisine, and the kitchen does both.

Guests say the staff walks you through the menu if it is new to you, and one visitor noted how ready they are to share their culture. Another family got a nesting doll for their goddaughter to play with and a dessert from grandma at the end of the meal. The room has a long bar, hanging lamps with trailing plants and red tablecloths.

  • Made freshReviewers call the food fresh and flavorful, and the vareniki are homemade.
  • Explained kindlyNew to Georgian food? The servers help you pick.
  • Room for a crowdCatering and private parties, plus an open-air patio guests mention.
