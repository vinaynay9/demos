The people

A family place on Race Track Road

Ellie's sits at 12008 Race Track Rd in Tampa, behind hand-lettered Breakfast and Lunch window signs. Inside it is a hometown diner: booths, tables with a colourful print cloth and locals enjoying their food.

Guests mention their servers by name. Diane, one wrote, was running around like a 20 year old. Georgia brought a Spanish-speaking coworker over so a guest's mom could follow the menu, and told them Ellie's is a family business. Parking is easy and kids are welcome.

  • Fair pricesGuests say it is better priced than anything in the area.
  • Big platesMassive portions, in one guest's words.
  • Friendly crewServers who keep checking that you're taken care of.
