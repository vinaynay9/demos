The pit

A tandoor in the Town and Country plaza

Tikka Masalaa opened on the 4th of July 2019 in Town and Country, at 10029 W Hillsborough Ave. The menu covers North and South Indian, vegetarian and non-vegetarian, with Indo Chinese dishes and a clay oven turning out tandoori lamb, seafood, chicken, breads and vegetables.

It sits in a row of shops, and one visitor said to go inside and take a look. Guests mention the tasteful decor, relaxing music, quick food and a waiter who looks after the table. Curries come with more than enough rice, and leftovers go home in a doggie bag.
