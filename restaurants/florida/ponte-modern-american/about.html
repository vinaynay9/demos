The second coming of Cafe Ponte

Ponte is the newly built successor to Cafe Ponte, which served Tampa Bay for over 18 years. Chef Chris Ponte trained at Le Cordon Bleu in Paris and blends Modern American cooking with French and Italian influences.

It is a family business: Chris runs The Ponte Group with his wife Michelle and their sons JT and Andy Mahoney. Guests mention the big chandelier at the entrance, white tablecloths and service that is attentive without hovering.

  • Steaks and seafoodWhat the kitchen is known for.
  • Tableside serviceSoup poured at the table, caviar and champagne from a guéridon.
  • Made for occasionsBirthdays are a common reason guests give.
