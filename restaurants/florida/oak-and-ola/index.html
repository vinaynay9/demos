Kitchen notes

Friends who love food, drink and company

Oak & Ola began with a group of close friends and longtime restaurateurs who wanted to build a local, independent EuroAmerican restaurant inside the Armature Works in North Tampa Heights.

The menu changes seasonally and the drinks are hand crafted. Guests mention the bar, the decor and the attention to detail, and they name the servers who looked after them.

  • Changes with the seasonsFood and drinks are thoughtfully sourced and hand crafted.
  • Looked afterGuests name the servers and bartenders who took care of them.
