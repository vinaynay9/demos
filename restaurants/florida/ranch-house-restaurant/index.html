Kitchen notes

The longhorn on Hillsborough

Ranch House is an old-fashioned family-style restaurant serving traditional American breakfast and lunch classics. The round longhorn badge says Since 1965, and the red sign over the door on West Hillsborough Avenue is easy to spot at the end of its strip mall, with parking right out front.

Inside there are booths, wood tables and wagon-wheel light fixtures. Guests mention friendly staff, cups that never went empty and prices they call fair. One family staying in Tampa skipped the chains on purpose and left satisfied.

  • Full plates, fair pricesGuests call the prices reasonable and leave full.
  • Friendly serviceServers who are prompt, warm and keep the coffee full.
  • Real breakfast and lunchWaffles, omelettes, burgers and burritos made the traditional way.
