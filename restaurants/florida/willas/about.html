Our story

A neighborhood spot in North Hyde Park

Willa's is a neighborhood restaurant and bar in North Hyde Park, serving familiar favorites, cocktails and wines, with everything made in-house and seasonal additions from the kitchen team.

Next door, Willa's Provisions is the corner coffee shop for specialty coffee and fresh-baked pastries. Guests describe a kind team, a chill atmosphere and a DJ spinning vinyl.
