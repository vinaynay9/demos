Our story

Forty years on S Howard Ave

Bella's Italian Cafe has served Tampa for 40 years, and the 1986 on the red-and-black sign on the plates says where it started. The dining room has classic booths and red-top tables, and the building has a long white-railed porch.

Guests mention the service as often as the food: a server who made a first-timer feel welcome, a waitress quick with every request, a staff that takes great care of you. Families and friends come for chicken parm, Alfredo, pizza and a drink from the bar.

  • Made from scratchReviewers say the noodles and sauce are made in house.
  • Friendly serviceServers described as welcoming, attentive and quick.
  • Plenty to choosePasta, pizza, antipasti, wine and beer, and vegan options.
