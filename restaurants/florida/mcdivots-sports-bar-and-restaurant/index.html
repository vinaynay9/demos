One of Westchase's originals

McDivot's was born from a passion for a bar and restaurant that works for families, friends and sports fans alike. It is one of Westchase's original restaurants, on Countryway Blvd, serving wings to the Tampa Bay area for more than 25 years.

The menu comes from John "Popa" Castaneda, one of the founders, who put his recipes and his friendly face into the place for 25 years. Popa's Red Chili, his original recipe, has been on the menu since 1998. Guests say the room is packed on football Sundays, with just about every game on.

  • Screens everywhereGuests say you can see the game from any seat.
