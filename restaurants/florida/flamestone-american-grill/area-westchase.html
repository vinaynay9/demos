What we cook

Fire, stone and a full bar on Tampa Road.

Tableside guacamole and starters

Guacamole made at your table in a molcajete, plus stone-oven flatbreads, a tuna tower, calamari and a white cheddar spinach dip.

Steaks, ribs and seafood

Angus beef grilled over an open flame, 30 day aged and hand cut here, plus baby back ribs, bourbon glazed salmon and fresh seafood delivered daily.

Bar, wine and Sunday brunch

A full liquor bar, hand-crafted cocktails, 150 wines with 30 by the glass, and Sunday brunch with bubbles and beignets.
