Meet the crew

Fire and stone, on Tampa Road

FlameStone is a casually sophisticated American grill in Oldsmar that cooks with nature's most basic elements: fire and stone. Steaks go over an open flame, flatbreads come out of the stone oven, and the guacamole is made tableside in a traditional molcajete with Haas avocados.

The steaks are Angus beef, 30 day aged and hand cut on premises. Behind the bar sit a full liquor bar and a 150 bottle wine list with 30 by the glass. Guests talk about the room as much as the plate: a business lunch where you can hear each other, a table of five that grew to eight without a fuss, and a server named Katt who made a solo diner feel looked after.
