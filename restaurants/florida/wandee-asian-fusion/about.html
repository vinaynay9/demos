Our story

A pink neon sign on a woven wall

Wandee Asian Fusion is a Thai restaurant at 5915 Memorial Hwy in Tampa. Paper umbrellas hang from the ceiling and a white blossom tree stands in the dining room.

Guests describe a small place with beautiful decor, friendly servers and food that looks as good as it tastes. Drinks arrive with a bear or dog shaped ice cube.

  • A huge menuA reviewer says any craving gets answered.
  • Vegetarian optionsGuests point out there are plenty.
  • Welcoming serviceDescribed as friendly, attentive and fast.
