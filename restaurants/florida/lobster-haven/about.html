Hello, we're

A New England shanty in Tampa

Lobster Haven sits on West Hillsborough Ave in a plain strip-mall storefront with hand-painted windows and a lobster on the sign. Inside, guests find a casual, shanty-style room with a bar, and a fish market side where you can pick up fish spread and dessert bars on your way out.

Their own words: everything starts with the catch, simple, fresh seafood rooted in tradition and made to feel like home by the water. Guests say the same. One family took their dad for a lobster dinner and he loved it. Another had never opened a freshly cooked lobster before and left pleasantly surprised by the price.
