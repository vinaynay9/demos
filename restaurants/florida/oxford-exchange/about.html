The people

An 1891 building, reimagined

Oxford Exchange opened on September 24, 2012, inside an 1891 building across the street from what was once Henry B. Plant's Tampa Bay Hotel. Brother and sister Blake Casper and Allison Adams brought it back after decades of vacancy, restoring the century old brickwork.

It began with a bookstore and an office, and grew into a restaurant, a shop and a Commerce Club inspired by the historic clubs and libraries of London. Guests call out the dark wood, the checkerboard floors and the staff who take care of them.
