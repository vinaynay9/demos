Raised up, like a vintage

Élevage grew out of the traditions of Bern's Steak House and sits inside the Epicurean Hotel on South Howard Avenue in SoHo. The name comes from the winemaker's term for raising a vintage until it is right, and the kitchen treats an old recipe the same way: one ingredient at a time, until the plate recalls the past and lives fully in the present.

Executive Chef Jonathan Atanacio leads the kitchen, with nearly 20 years of cooking behind him, much of it in New York City and most recently at Bern's. Guests mention the decor, the wine cellar of vintage bottles, hot and fresh plates, and servers like Carly who make a night easy.
