Meet your mechanic

On West Waters since 2008

Twilight Auto Repair is family owned and operated, working out of Suite B at 5638 W Waters Ave. The shop focuses on European vehicles, and the lot and bays show it: a Mercedes with its hood up, a Mini on the lift, a BMW M3 and an orange VW.

Customers mention Mike and Jane by name. They say the crew is welcoming, patient and honest, explains the process and the cost up front, and worked around a busy schedule. One customer picked up one car and dropped off another on the same visit.
