The crew

A Tampa shop for Land Rovers

Mitanix is a family owned auto repair shop at 4420 N Hale Ave in Tampa. It works on Land Rovers, including classic models, with new and used parts.

Owners trust it with a 1989 Defender D110, a 94 Range Rover Classic and a 1969 Series IIa. One owner said Moreb is the best Land Rover mechanic in Tampa. Another said the team is generous with its knowledge, especially for people new to classic Land Rovers.
