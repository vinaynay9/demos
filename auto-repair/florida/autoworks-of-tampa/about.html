Who's under the hood

Full service, on N Nebraska Ave

AutoWorks of Tampa is a full-service preventive maintenance and repair center on N Nebraska Ave, with over 15 years of experience. Owner Bob Noriega and the team repair domestic and foreign vehicles and handle scheduled maintenance for cars, SUVs, trucks and fleets.

Drivers mention the organized front office, the tidy waiting room and a detailed invoice emailed after the job. One brought in a Land Rover and a Dodge Ram, got a plain answer the next day, and was told exactly what needed to be done before any work began.
