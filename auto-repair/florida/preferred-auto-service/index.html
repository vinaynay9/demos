The shop

A shop on N Thatcher Ave

Preferred Auto Service sits at 4404 N Thatcher Ave in Tampa, in a metal shop building with a hand-hung banner out front. Its own sign carries Porsche, Mercedes-Benz, Land Rover, Volkswagen, Jaguar and Volvo badges.

Reviewers name Terry again and again: reliable, efficient, trustworthy, and the only person one customer will bring his car to. Another had Matt check a car before buying it from a dealer, and has used the shop for routine maintenance since.
