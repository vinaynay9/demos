The harness

Since 2005, on North Florida Ave

Heights Auto Imports looks after Audi, BMW, Land Rover, Jaguar, Mercedes-Benz, MINI, Porsche, Volvo and other European cars from a shop on North Florida Avenue in Tampa. Maintenance, brakes, suspension, transmission, electrical and computer diagnostics are all done under one roof.

Owners keep naming the front desk. Teresa explains the work without pressure and keeps you updated, and techs like Kyle and Ray get credit for finding hard problems on Audis and Porsches. After each service you get a digital inspection with photos and videos, an itemized invoice, and a prioritized list of future maintenance.

  • Diagnose firstRoot cause confirmed before any work begins.
  • 3 years, 36,000 milesWarranty on all parts and labor.
  • No hidden feesThe menu price is the ticket price.
