Red bays on Nebraska Ave

Fix To Go Auto Care Center is at 6306 N Nebraska Ave in Tampa: a row of red-trimmed bays with Oil Change, Tune-Up, Tires, Brakes and Alignment lettered over the doors. Their site says they have served the Tampa community for over 15 years.

Customers talk about how they are treated. One came in for an oil change on a tight budget and the staff worked with them on the price.
