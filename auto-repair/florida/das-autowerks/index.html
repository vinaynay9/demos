Who we are

Tampa's VW and Audi people

DAS Autowerks is a locally owned shop at 4014 West South Avenue in Tampa. The sign out front says performance parts and repairs, servicing all makes and models, specializing in VW and Audi, and the reviews back it up.

Owners bring in a 2020 GTI with a failed engine, a Passat they have never had looked at, an Audi that needs programming. Josh and the team confirm the problem, explain it straight and tell you what it costs before the work starts. The shop also follows up after the visit to see how the car is running.
