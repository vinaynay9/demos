Good to know

Questions people ask

Do you work on Volkswagen and Audi?

Yes, they are our specialty. We also work on other European and domestic cars, trucks, vans and SUVs.

Is there a warranty on the work?

Yes, the shop lists a 2 year / 24,000 mile nationwide warranty. Ask us for the details when you call.

Do you have after hours drop-off?

Yes. Call ahead and we will tell you how it works.

Do you offer a discount?

The shop lists 10% off for military and first responders. Ask when you book.

Can you handle a check engine light?

Yes. We diagnose it, explain what is going on and give you the options.
