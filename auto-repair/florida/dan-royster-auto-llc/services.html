Good to know

Questions people ask

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday 8 AM to 5 PM. Closed Saturday and Sunday.

Do you work on Porsches?

Yes. Porsche service and repair is the shop's specialty, and customers name Dan as their go-to mechanic.

Can you inspect a used car before I buy it?

Yes. One customer had a used car inspected before buying it for a new driver and got a report on what needed work.

What if my car needs work you cannot do?

Dan will say so. One owner reported he helped load the car onto a transport truck to another shop.

How much will it cost?

We do not post prices. Call or send your car's details and Dan will explain the repair and quote it before work starts.
