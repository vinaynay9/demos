A family-run Tampa shop.

At the bench

Jose, Jaime and the crew

JJ Mechanic Corp is a general auto repair shop on West Linebaugh Avenue in Tampa, run by Jose and Jaime Murillo. Customers ask for them by name, and one regular drives in from Orlando.

We work with personal attention and transparency. We inspect your vehicle, explain the problem and help you make an informed decision.
