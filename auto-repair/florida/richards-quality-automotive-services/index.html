The shop

Open bays on East Hillsborough

Richard's Quality Automotive Services is an open-air shop at 503 E Hillsborough Ave in Tampa, with lifts under a long awning and banners over each bay. It works on Asian and domestic cars, trucks and SUVs.

Reviewers keep coming back to Tom and his team. One has brought three cars here over ten years and calls the shop trustworthy. A mother in Virginia had her son's car towed in and left glad she did. Another driver said choosing Richard's saved over four grand, with no pressure to push unneeded repairs.

OUR POLICY

  1. No upsell. Drivers say they were not pushed into repairs they did not need.
  2. Quick and communicative. One driver called the work quick, clear and affordable.
  3. Second opinions. One shop said leak, Richard's inspected and found none.
