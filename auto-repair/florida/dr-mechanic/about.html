About

The people behind Dr. Mechanic.

The bench

A family shop on Fowler Ave

Dr. Mechanic is a family-owned and operated shop in Tampa Bay, open since 2004 at 1102 E Fowler Ave. The mechanics are ASE certified, the parts are OEM-certified, and the work comes with a 1-year warranty on parts and labor. English and Spanish both spoken here.

Customers keep saying the same thing: they get told what is wrong, why it matters and what it costs. One young woman who dreads car shops was taken into the garage and shown exactly what was going on. Another was offered several options on an engine that would have to be rebuilt, and Andrew even drove her to the DMV to pick up a title.
