The shop

Do it right, or do it twice

Franklin Car Care is a repair shop at 5809 Little River Dr in Tampa. The shop's own saying is "do it right or do it twice", so the technicians aim to fix the problem the first time, whatever it is.

Reviewers name Jeff and Trent again and again: friendly, honest about what the car needs, and quick to get a Honda, a GMC Acadia or a Toyota back on the road. Repairs are explained, and an estimate comes before work begins.

The shop is a NAPA AutoCare Center, so qualifying repairs carry a 24 month, 24,000 mile warranty that other NAPA shops honor if you are traveling.
