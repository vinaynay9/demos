Who answers the phone

The shop with the black and yellow sign

Import Auto Center sits at 2110 W Hillsborough Ave in Tampa, a pink building with two lift bays and a lot of cars out front. The sign lists the work: diagnostics and repair, auto electric, air suspension, A/C, brakes and oil changes, and heating and cooling.

The same lot sells quality pre-owned vehicles, many of them lease returns and new car trades, with financing options and a way to sell us your car. One customer says they have been coming since the shop opened. Another thanks Fatima for explaining everything.
