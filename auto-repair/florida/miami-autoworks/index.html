Good questions

Do you work with my insurance company?

We work with all major insurance companies, so call us after an accident and we will help you get started.

Do estimates cost anything?

No. Estimates are free, and we also offer towing and shuttle service.

What do I do while my car is being worked on?

Drop it off and we will shuttle you back to your home or office, or relax in our waiting area.

What kinds of cars do you work on?

Domestic vehicles plus European, Japanese, German and other foreign makes, including Lexus, Honda, BMW, Mercedes, Porsche, Acura, Volvo, Corvette and Nissan.

When are you open?

Monday through Friday from 8 AM to 5 PM. We are closed on weekends.
