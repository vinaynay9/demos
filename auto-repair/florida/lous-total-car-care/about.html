About Lou's

An auto repair shop in Tampa that explains the work.

Rest area

Lou's Total Car Care

Lou's is an auto repair shop at 8317 Rustic Dr in Tampa. It is the yellow tile building with the red stripe and the phone number painted on the front.

Reviewers name Manny, Jon and Orlando, and thank Lou. They say the crew is friendly, explains what is necessary and does not push work you don't need.

One driver had two problems and got one fix. A dad called from out of state about his daughter's failed alternator, and Orlando had the car fixed within a couple of hours.
