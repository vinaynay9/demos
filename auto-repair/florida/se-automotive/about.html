Meet Sam and the team

SE Automotive is a working garage on West Alva Street in Tampa, with lifts, a full tool chest and cars lined up out front. Customers talk about Sam by name: knowledgeable, fair on price and eager to please.

One owner of a 2012 Volvo C70 had major work done and got it back with no shake or vibration, tracking well on the interstate. Another has brought in his cars for over 15 years and sends friends and family. A customer who works at a Tampa dealership says Sam fixes what the dealership mechanics can't.
