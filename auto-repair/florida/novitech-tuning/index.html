What we do

  • Oil change service

    Along with the oil change, fluids are refilled if needed and tire pressures are set.

  • Scheduled maintenance

    Staying on top of your maintenance schedule helps avoid breakdowns and costly repairs, and protects the value of your car.

  • Comprehensive diagnostic

    Check engine light on? We track down the real cause so you don't pay for parts you didn't need.

  • Wheel alignment

    Alignments on a Hunter Engineering RX Hawkeye system with ride height targets, for even tire wear and a centered steering wheel.

  • Tire services

    Rotation and balancing, flat repairs, and mounting and balancing factory or aftermarket wheels with low profile tires.

  • Engine repair and rebuilding

    From head gaskets and oil pumps to compression tests and full rebuilds, engine work is a regular job in our shop.

  • Engine performance

    More performance, done safely and without giving up drivability or smoothness.

Good questions

Do you only work on European cars?

No. European cars are our specialty, but we also service most import and domestic vehicles, including Lexus, Nissan, Honda, Toyota, Subaru, Ford, Dodge and Chevy.

Do you accept extended warranties?

We accept most extended warranties. Bring us your policy's contact information and one of our service advisors will take care of the rest.

Where are you located?

We're about 5 miles west of exit 19 (SW 120th St.) on the Florida Turnpike.

Is there somewhere to wait while my car is worked on?

Yes. We have a comfortable customer lounge with a flat screen TV.
