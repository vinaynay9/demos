(04) About

A modern shop on N Himes Ave

Rennen Imports describes itself as a dealership alternative for European auto repair in Tampa. The shop is modern and secure, and its factory trained technicians handle minor and major repairs and factory scheduled maintenance.

Drivers praise how the team explains the work. One BMW X3 owner came for a second opinion after a diagnosis that felt wrong, and Steve, Audra and the team found and explained the real issue with a quick turnaround.
