Who's under the hood

The shop on W South Ave

Ricky Auto Repair Corp is an independent shop on West South Avenue in Tampa, with the shop name across the front of the building and the services painted on the sign by the road.

Local companies send their service trucks here for repairs and maintenance. Regular customers say the work is quick, the pricing is fair and the staff is friendly. Ask for Richard the master mechanic, and the office speaks both English and Spanish.
