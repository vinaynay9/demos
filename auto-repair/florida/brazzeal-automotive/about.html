About us

The same corner since 1980

Brazzeal Automotive is a family-owned and operated shop at 5002 W Kennedy Blvd in Tampa. The goal is to fix your vehicle right the first time and to explain the work and your repair options in detail, drawing on more than 40 years at this location.

Customers mention Chris and Logan by name. They praise the video inspection, the lock box for after-hours key pick-up and drop-off, and the waiting area with comfy seats, a big TV, coffee and snacks. The shop was honored with the 2025 Top Shop Award.

  1. Honest evaluationsA driver was told her brakes were good for at least another year.
  2. Easy on your scheduleVideo inspection, online payment and after-hours key drop-off.
  3. Warranty behind itA 3 year, 36k mile Nationwide Parts Plus warranty.
