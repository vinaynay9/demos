Good to know

Questions people ask

Will you tell me if I do not need the repair?

That is how customers describe it. One driver was told her brakes were fine for at least another year.

Can I drop the car off after hours?

Yes. After-hours pick-up and drop-off are available, and one customer's keys waited in a lock box.

Is there a warranty?

Yes, a 3 year, 36k mile Nationwide Parts Plus warranty.

Do you work on fleets?

Yes. Fleet brakes, drive train, electrical, engine work and tires are all listed.

What can I do while I wait?

There is a comfortable waiting area with snacks, drinks, a TV and Wi-Fi.

Which vehicles do you work on?

Most cars, SUVs, light trucks and hybrids, domestic and import.
