Meet Marpeña Auto Repair

We're a family-owned shop at 5301 N Nebraska Ave in Tampa. Customers tell us Cesar, his father, mother and sister all treat them like family, and that's how we like it.

The sign out front says all types of repair, and the bays say tune-ups, brakes, lube and oil change. Customers also come for engine repair, alignments and tie rods.

Customers say the turnaround is quick and the price is fair. One said we had his wife's car back the same day, even though the shop was busy. Thank you for trusting us.
